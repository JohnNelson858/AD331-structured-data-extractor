Hi, I'm John Nelson. This is my resilient invoice extraction project for AD 331. The goal is to turn messy documents into records that downstream software can safely validate and process.

The repository separates schemas, ingestion, provider integration, extraction, recovery, and the web interface. The README diagram maps ingestion, decoding, validation, and recovery.

In schemas.py, an invoice contains nested line items. Quantities must be positive integers. Invoice numbers follow a pattern, currencies use an allowed list, and money uses Decimal for accurate arithmetic. Before validators normalize explicit dates and currency-formatted amounts. The model validators check each line, the subtotal, tax, and grand total within a two-cent tolerance. Unknown fields and ambiguous dates are rejected.
